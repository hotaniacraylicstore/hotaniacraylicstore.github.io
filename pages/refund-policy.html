Refund & Return Policy

At HOTANI ACRYLIC STORE, we want you to be satisfied with your purchase. Please read our return and refund guidelines before placing an order.

1. Return Requests

If you receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect product, please contact us as soon as possible after receiving your order.

2. Damaged or Incorrect Products

If your order arrives damaged or you receive a different product, please share clear photographs and your order details with our support team. We will review your request and guide you about the available solution.

3. Customized Products

Customized and personalized products may have different return or refund eligibility because they are prepared according to customer requirements. Please confirm all names, numbers, spellings, and design details before placing a customized order.

4. Refund Approval

Refunds are reviewed individually. If a refund is approved, our team will inform you about the refund method and expected processing time.

5. Exchanges

Exchanges may be considered depending on the product condition, availability, and reason for the request.

6. How to Contact Us

To request a return, exchange, or refund, please contact HOTANI ACRYLIC STORE through our official contact channels and provide your order details.

7. Policy Updates

We may update this policy when necessary. Any changes will be published on this page.
