Shipping Policy

Welcome to HOTANI ACRYLIC STORE. This page explains how we handle order processing and delivery.

1. Order Processing

Orders are processed after confirmation. Processing time may vary depending on product availability and customization requirements.

2. Delivery Areas

Delivery availability depends on the customer's location and courier service coverage.

3. Delivery Time

Estimated delivery time may vary depending on the destination, courier service, weather conditions, and other circumstances. We will share available delivery information when confirming your order.

4. Shipping Charges

Shipping charges, if applicable, will be communicated to the customer before the order is finalized.

5. Order Tracking

If tracking information is available, it will be shared with the customer after dispatch.

6. Incorrect Address

Customers are responsible for providing a complete and correct delivery address and contact details.

7. Delayed Deliveries

HOTANI ACRYLIC STORE is not responsible for delays caused by courier services, weather, or circumstances beyond our control.

8. Contact Us

For shipping-related questions, please contact us through our official store contact channels.
